Privacy

Updated 11 September 2026

Decal Fix OS is the job-order app that Decal Fix Printing Services uses at its counter and in its workshop. Spawn Solutions builds and runs it for the shop. This page says what it keeps, where, and what it does with your Google account when the shop connects one.

What the app keeps

  • Job orders: the customer's name and contact number, the items, sizes, quantities, prices, notes, delivery date, payments and their receipt numbers, and for government orders the account and purchase order number.
  • The names of the shop's staff and which of them took each order or payment. Staff PINs are stored only as a one-way hash.

These are stored in the shop's own database and can be seen only by the shop's signed-in staff.

Google Drive

When the shop connects its Google account, the app asks for one permission: drive.file. With it, the app can see and change only the files and folders it created itself in the shop's Drive - the job-order folders and the files uploaded through the app. It cannot open anything else in that Drive, and it never reads anyone's Gmail.

If the shop shares an order's folder with an artist, Google sends that artist an invitation. The app keeps the artist's Google address only to share that folder with them and to remove their access when they leave.

Decal Fix OS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell or rent any of this information, or share it with advertisers.
  • It shows no ads and does not track you across other websites.
  • It does not use Google data for anything other than keeping the shop's job-order files.

Stopping access

The shop can disconnect the app from its Google account at any time: Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Decal Fix OS → Delete all connections.

Questions

Ask at the Decal Fix Printing Services counter in Dipolog City, or write to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Decal Fix OS.
